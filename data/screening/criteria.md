# Relevance criteria (agreed defaults, CW 2026-10-06)

Research question (from Notes_review): methodological approaches and best
practices for uncertainty quantification in decision-making frameworks, to
assess the value of information and inform interventions or policies.

Judge each record on TITLE + ABSTRACT only.

## INCLUDE if ALL are true
1. Research output (article, chapter, book, report, thesis). Not news, syllabus, course notes, catalogue.
2. Decision context: a choice between options (policy, intervention, investment, management, allocation).
3. Uncertainty is treated explicitly (probabilistic / Bayesian / simulation / value of information / risk analysis / sensitivity analysis / expert judgement).
4. A method to support the decision is described, applied or evaluated (not only mentioned).

## EXCLUDE if ANY is true
- Decisions or uncertainty only mentioned in passing.
- Descriptive or behavioural study with no decision-support method.
- Pure statistics / machine learning / engineering with no decision context.
- Not a research output.

## UNSURE
Abstract too short or too vague to judge. Goes to full-text check.

## Decisions (CW, 2026-10-06)
- Case or sample study required? NO.
- Deterministic decision analysis (no uncertainty treatment)? OUT (criterion 3).
- Language: not checked for now. Records are mostly English; sort the others out later.
